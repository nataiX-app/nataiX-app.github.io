Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I install updates?
A: Updates are delivered automatically through AltStore PAL. Check the AltStore app for available updates.

Q: The app crashes or won't open. What should I do?
A: Make sure your device is running iOS 17 or later and that your AltStore PAL subscription/source is active. Try reinstalling the app if the issue persists.
