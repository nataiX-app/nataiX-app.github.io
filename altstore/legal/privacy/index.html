KeepTether

Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 28, 2026

1. Overview

Protecting your privacy is a top priority at KeepTether ("the App"). This Privacy Policy outlines how the App collects, uses, and manages information.

The App does not send or store any personal information or location data to external servers. All data processing occurs entirely on your device locally.

2. Information Collection & Usage

To provide its core functionality, the App requests and uses the following permissions and device features:

  • Location Data (GPS / Location Services): Used solely to maintain Wi-Fi tethering connections and detect movement from a reference point for movement alerts (e.g., 50 meters). To function while running in the background, "Always Allow" permission may be requested. Location data is processed strictly in real-time on your device and is never stored externally or transmitted to third parties.
  • Network Connection Status: The App uses status monitoring (e.g., NWPathMonitor) to detect connection types such as Tethering, Wi-Fi, and Cellular. This is used to trigger disconnection alerts and auto-ping features. Communication contents and personal logs are neither captured nor stored.
  • Periodic Ping Transmission: To prevent iOS Wi-Fi tethering from entering sleep mode, the App sends periodic HTTP request pings (to Apple's standard connection verification endpoint) at user-configured intervals (default: 90 seconds). No identifiable user information is attached to these requests.
  • Notifications & Local Preferences (UserDefaults): Local notifications are used to alert you when movement is detected or when tethering disconnects. User settings (such as ping intervals) are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (UserDefaults).

3. Third-Party Sharing & External Data Transfer

The App does not sell, share, or transfer any data or usage history to third parties, except where required by law. The App contains no third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks.

4. Data Retention & Management

All configuration data and operational logs generated by the App are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the App will completely remove all associated local data permanently.

5. Disclaimer & Battery Usage

Because the App executes background tasks (such as location updates and network keep-alive requests), continuous operation may impact battery life. Furthermore, background updates are subject to iOS system power management controls and network availability.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in app features or legal requirements. Any revisions will become effective immediately upon posting to this page.
